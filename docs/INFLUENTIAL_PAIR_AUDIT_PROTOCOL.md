# Stage 7 protocol for the wider-only dense-area pairs

## Audit population

Stage 6 compared a primary five-point rule with a rule that allowed larger differences in combined tree and rangeland, built cover, and other cover. Link the two frozen pair tables by date and ordered endpoints. Audit the nine records present only under the wider rule. They represent three distinct spatial pairs in two blocks. The selected records and block omission use the Stage 6 findings, which makes this a follow-up sensitivity analysis.

## Image and temperature review

Archive source-resolution Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 windows for the eight pertinent overpasses. Use the optical red, green, blue and near-infrared bands from the same products as surface temperature, along with `QA_PIXEL`, `QA_RADSAT`, `ST_B10`, `ST_QA`, and `ST_CDIST`. Keep the native 30 m delivery grid and original digital numbers. Extract the corresponding 10 m annual Esri labels and common WorldCover study-land mask. Reproduce original cell temperature, valid land coverage, and stricter temperature screens using the original footprints and archived class counts. The 30 m delivery grid samples a coarser thermal measurement. [Landsat Level 2 product guide](https://www.usgs.gov/media/files/landsat-8-9-collection-2-level-2-science-product-guide) · [USGS quality bands](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-quality-assessment-bands)

Apply the original mask for fill, cloud, cloud shadow, cirrus, snow and the 15–70 °C range. Summarize temperature uncertainty and distance to the nearest mapped cloud among retained study-land centers. Compare cover shares on the full classified land domain and the part contributing to the original temperature. Repeat the pair temperature comparisons after applying the Stage 3 distance and uncertainty screens. Require cloud distance of at least 0.50 km, reported pixel uncertainty of at most 4 K or 2 K, and at least 60% study-land coverage at both endpoints for the corresponding comparison. The QA uncertainty band describes the input retrieval, so use it to compare quality across endpoints. Calculate Landsat NDVI on clear, unsaturated land using scaled reflectances and report the covered fraction. Use the original optical images to interpret visible surface texture and vegetation. [USGS surface temperature](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-surface-temperature) · [USGS surface reflectance](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-surface-reflectance)

Inspect one near-date Sentinel-2 Level-2A image per distinct spatial pair for 10 m context. For a repeated pair, use the Landsat date that covers the most distinct pairs and the earlier date to break a tie. Search the same Sentinel-2 tile within 30 days on either side of each reference. Choose the acquisition nearest in calendar days with at least 80% scene-classified clear land at each endpoint, breaking ties by higher minimum clear fraction and then product ID. Use the scene classification and original optical bands. Record the time offset from the Landsat image and any visible ambiguity around individual mapped classes. The optical review does not supply new thermal measurements. [Copernicus Sentinel-2 processing](https://sentiwiki.copernicus.eu/web/s2-processing)

## Omit both blocks

Reproduce the Stage 6 estimates from the primary and wider matches. Exclude every pair in either of the two focal 4 km blocks from both frozen lists. Give each remaining overpass equal total weight under the Stage 6 rules. Compare the original and block-omitted matched estimates, the retained dates and pair IDs, and whether the covered-change adjustment remains feasible under the Stage 6 rank and block checks. Also calculate the matched point differences with original overpass weights carried forward to show the contribution of weight changes.

Use the Stage 6 fixed-weight geographic bootstrap for the intervals of each estimate. To summarize the change, draw original 4 km blocks with replacement and apply the same block multiplicities to estimates before and after omission. Use the weights defined by each estimator. Exclude draws without any retained block and require full rank in both fits for an adjusted change. Report the point change as the omitted-sample estimate minus the full-sample estimate. The intervals describe sensitivity to geography in this observed sample. [Stage 6 methods](DENSE_TREE_SWAP_PROTOCOL.md#five-point-temperature-contrast)
